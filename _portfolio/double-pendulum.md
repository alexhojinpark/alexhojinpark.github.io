## Highlights

- A double pendulum whose position shapes the sound in real time
- Graphics and [double-pendulum physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Double_pendulum) built from scratch
- Audio generation whose frequency and amplitude follow the pendulum, with sine, square and sawtooth variants

Inspired by *The Double Pendulum Fractal*. I learned graphics, physics and audio integration with SDL in C++.
