## Highlights

**DSP:** Low-cut, peak and high-cut filters
**GUI:** Spectrum analyzer, responsive curve, bypass buttons, custom theme

This was my first JUCE project. I built it by following a tutorial and the JUCE docs, and it gave me a solid grounding in the Plug-In Processor and Plug-In Editor. I finished it over Christmas, so I gave it a festive theme.
